This account is for tax-exempt organizations only: schools, districts, nonprofits, and churches with a valid Florida Consumer's Certificate of Exemption (Form DR-14). We keep a copy of your certificate on file, and orders must be paid directly by the organization (school check, purchase order, or organization card).
